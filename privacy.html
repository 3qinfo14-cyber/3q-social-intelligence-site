Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 30, 2026

3Q Social Intelligence is a social media analytics application. This policy explains how the application collects, processes, stores and protects information.

1. Information We Collect

When a user connects an authorized social media account, the application may collect:

  • Social media account identifiers
  • Display names and profile information
  • Available follower and account statistics
  • Published posts and video information
  • Views, likes, comments and share counts
  • OAuth credentials needed for integration

2. How Information Is Used

Collected information is used to provide account analytics, content performance monitoring and workspace-based reporting.

The application does not use connected account access to publish, edit or delete social media content.

3. TikTok Integration

TikTok users may authorize the application through TikTok's official authentication process.

With the user's permission, the application accesses available profile information, account statistics and public video performance information.

TikTok information is used for analytics and reporting within the authorized workspace.

4. Security

The application uses account authentication and workspace access controls. OAuth credentials are intended to be stored securely using encryption.

No online system can guarantee absolute security.

5. Third-Party Platforms

Connected platforms such as TikTok, Facebook, Instagram and YouTube operate under their own privacy policies.

6. Disconnect and Data Deletion

Users may disconnect supported accounts through the application.

Disconnecting an account removes its stored connection credentials but may preserve previously imported analytics.

Users may request deletion of their stored data by contacting the service operator through the email below.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application or its data practices change.
